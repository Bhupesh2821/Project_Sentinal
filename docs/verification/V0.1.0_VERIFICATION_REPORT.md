**Date:** 2026-10-21. **Status:** working macOS Vision Core delivered with real camera, native desktop, CPU/MPS inference and local metadata verified. Apple M4 MacBook Air, 16 GB RAM, macOS 26.6.2 arm64; Python 3.12.15; Ultralytics 8.4.183; PyTorch 2.14.1; torchvision 0.29.1; OpenCV 4.14.0; Qt/PySide6 Essentials/Addons 6.12.0; lap 0.5.13. Later approved native execution outside that restricted context successfully displayed Cocoa windows, used MPS and opened the built-in camera. **The earlier restrictions are not current native-device blockers.**

The model is official local `yolo11n.pt`, SHA256 `0ebbc80d4a7680d14987a577cd21342b65ecfd94632bd9a8da63ae6417644ee1`. Its 80 COCO classes include person, backpack, handbag and suitcase. Model/dependency installation is the only intentional download stage. License and class details are in LICENSE_NOTES.md and docs/MODEL_CLASSES.json.

## Measured performance

Real OpenCV pedestrian video, 768 × 576, 90 processed frames per device/size. Values below exclude first-frame initialization; source decoding, detection and tracking are included in processing FPS. GUI paint and the desktop's deliberate 10 FPS file playback pacing are excluded.

| Inference size | CPU processing FPS | CPU mean vision latency | MPS processing FPS | MPS mean vision latency |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 320 | 34.58 | 27.76 ms | 55.15 | 17.13 ms |
| 480 | 22.05 | 44.27 ms | 46.80 | 20.31 ms |
| 640 | 13.45 | 73.23 ms | 37.15 | 25.71 ms |

These are observed processing capacities on one short public video, not fixed live-camera promises. During five brief camera control cycles at 640 inference / 640 × 480 capture, warmed cycles measured **7.36–13.00 processing FPS** and **51.51–100.89 ms** rolling vision latency; camera read overhead and a changing live scene differ from the recorded-video benchmark. Each cycle sampled only six displayed frames, so these are short acceptance measurements rather than a sustained thermal benchmark. The first cold camera cycle included initialization and had 623.29 ms rolling latency. Normal measured stop times were 68–105 ms.

The native video UI timer's p95 same-stage gap was 11.98 ms; its largest same-stage gap was 123.57 ms, including startup. The largest gap during first video loading/inference was 83.31 ms. The camera verifier recorded 1,133 timer callbacks with maximum same-stage gap 54.91 ms. This supports responsiveness in these runs, not a guarantee against all OS/driver stalls. Heavy Python library imports are deliberately performed before showing the window. The recorded native pre-window startup was 13.06 s, including 3.79 s of vision-library preparation. Initial cold MPS inference emitted an Ultralytics NMS time-limit advisory; the run completed with detections and no verification failure. Cold startup can be slow.

## Detection and tracking limits observed

The original three COCO128 development images were tested at 320/480/640 using class-matched IoU ≥ 0.5 against their supplied annotations:

| Image/category | 320 matches | 480 matches | 640 matches |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 260 / suitcase | 1 of 3 | 1 of 3 | 3 of 3 |
| 328 / backpack | 0 of 2 | 1 of 2 | 1 of 2 |
| 315 / handbag | 0 of 3 | 0 of 3 | 0 of 3 |

Suitcase image 260 also produced two unmatched suitcase boxes at 640. Original missed handbags remain in the report. Additional deliberately selected real photos demonstrated a backpack (image 257), suitcase (572), and handbag (532); the handbag box in image 532 matches an annotated handbag at approximately 0.949 IoU. Image 536 produced a handbag label but its box was just below the 0.5 matching cutoff; it is retained as an imperfect localization example. These selected examples demonstrate supported output paths, not calibrated accuracy. COCO128 comes from train2017 and may overlap model pretraining data.

Real moving pedestrian footage produced multiple simultaneous IDs and repeated IDs across frames. At size 640 in the original 60-frame CPU run, person ID 4 was absent between frames 17 and 35, and ID 15 had gaps of 6, 13 and 1 observations. These are visible detection/track interruptions. Without identity ground truth they cannot be counted as confirmed ID switches. Perfect tracking, long-occlusion recovery, cross-camera identity and moving-bag identity continuity have not been established.
