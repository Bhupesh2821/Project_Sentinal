# Licensed V0.2 demonstration inputs

`handgun_repeated_still.mp4` repeats the public-domain US Army M1911 photograph for eight seconds. It is a real photograph and genuine detector inference, but not moving-object footage or a tracking-quality benchmark. No detections or scores are embedded. The source record is `../v02-evaluation/handgun.source.json`.

`fire_repeated_still.mp4` (US Forest Service public domain) and `smoke_repeated_still.mp4` (Wikimedia photographer Brocken Inaglory, CC BY-SA 3.0; attribution/license in adjacent evaluation source JSON) likewise repeat licensed real photographs, resized to at most1280 pixels wide for eight seconds. They verify real inference and temporal integration only; no natural movement is claimed. Choose confidence .35 for this initial small calibration set.
