01 / ENGINEERING APPROACH

One platform. Clear responsibilities.

The camera and inference engine remain independent of the command center. Video capture and AI run locally; authorized incident metadata can travel over an authenticated connection.

01

Cameras at the edge

Explicit source selection, bounded frame buffering and independent connection health. Discovery finds compatible advertised camera services.

02

Events over predictions

Confidence, consecutive observations and persistence rules assess possible events. Duplicate suppression keeps repeated predictions from flooding operators.

03

People in control

Account-scoped incidents, human review and an audit history. A model score does not establish danger or intent.

02 / CAPABILITIES & LIMITS

Real inference. Explicit uncertainty.

Implemented

Vision Core

People, supported everyday objects, bags, bounding boxes and optional tracking IDs. Four ordinary model profiles offer different speed and quality trade-offs.

Experimental

Possible weapons, fire and smoke

Dedicated pretrained models produce real predictions. False positives and missed objects remain in evaluation; sharp objects and moving smoke are not reliably covered.

Connected prototype

Private command center

Signed edge events, persisted incident history, authenticated review and responsive desktop/mobile layouts. Hosted access requires a configured deployment.

Not implemented

Future intelligence

Unattended-item rules, GHS recognition and advanced event correlation require their own implementation and validation.

03 / ACTUAL APPLICATION

04 / OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Designed for supervised use.

Private by default. No camera starts from opening a dashboard. Evidence, recording and network delivery require explicit configuration.

Local vision. Continuous inference stays on a compatible edge device. No facial identification or automatic accusations.

Honest evidence. Performance comes from measured runs. This prototype is not certified emergency equipment.
